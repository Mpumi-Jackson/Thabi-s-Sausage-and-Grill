About Us

In the food industry, Thabi's Sausage and Grill is a company that was founded with the goal of turning a profit.

Our goals are to improve our clients' lives and facilitate the resolution of conflicts between vendors and well-established companies.

Maintaining a clean atmosphere is equally as important as making sure that we are well-known for our services in and around Pretoria and that many people choose to do business with us.

We possess the issued documentation and meet all the conditions listed below:

  • SARS has a CIPC registration.
  • Certification of Acceptability (COA) and a letter of good standing.
  • Documents on Fire.
  • Trade authorization from Tshwane.

The money will be used to expand the company, and each year a portion of our net earnings is given to the less fortunate.

Our Mission

To provide quality food to the community in a convenient and affordable way to enhance the quality of our customers' lives.

Our Philosophy and Vision

The philosophy of our business is "Don’t stop when you’re tired, stop when you’re done," because we know that it’s very normal to get tired of being an entrepreneur, especially when business is not blooming. So, if we make persistence our best friend, we can succeed over the coming years.

Our vision is to be the best and well-known food truck business in South Africa.

Our Goals

Our goal is to make our customers happy and make a profit. We aim to grow rapidly over the coming years and put our company name on the map.

Products & Services

Thabi’s Sausage and Grill offers a wide range of food products, including:

  • Burgers
  • Kota’s
  • Boerewors Rolls
  • Fries
  • Pap n Vleis (only served as a special)
  • Dagwood
  • Vegan Special (on Thursday)

All our foods are pork-free.

It’s safe to say our services are highly recommended.

We have worked with different types of events, catering to various customers. We are currently located at Varsity College and Menlyn Mall, running our food business daily.

We have worked at school events such as sports events, food fests, DA conferences, Dinho Café, Bikers festivals, and HomeComingEvents. We also offer catering services for birthday parties, weddings, and family events.

Thabi’s Sausage and Grill is currently working on getting the business on Uber Eats and MrD, which will allow us to scale the business optimally with quick and efficient services.

Please note that we accept card payments.
